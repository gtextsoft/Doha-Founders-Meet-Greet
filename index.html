Why this room

You don't come for slides. You come for the people in the seats.

The Meet & Greet is built for founders and chief executives who are ready to operate beyond their home market. Three reasons it's worth the trip to Doha.

01

Global expansion

Move past the theory of "going international." Spend the day with people who have already opened markets in the Gulf, Africa, Europe and North America — and learn the moves that actually worked.

02

High-level access

Proximity is the product. Sit alongside capital, operators and decision-makers in one curated room, hosted by the Stephen Akintayo Foundation in one of the world's fastest-moving business hubs.

03

Founder network

The conversations continue long after 1 August. Walk away with relationships you can call on — partners, co-investors and peers solving the same scale problems you are.
